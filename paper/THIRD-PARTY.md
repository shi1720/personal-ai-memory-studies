# Typesetting files

The ICML 2026 LaTeX style, bibliography style, algorithm, algorithmic and fancyhdr files were obtained unmodified from the official author-instructions archive:
https://media.icml.cc/Conferences/ICML2026/Styles/icml2026.zip

Their existing author and license notices are retained. The repository's code license does not replace those notices. Using this style does not mean that the paper was submitted to, reviewed by, or accepted at ICML. The named manuscript uses the preprint option.

## TMLR typesetting files

The unmodified `tmlr.sty` and `tmlr.bst` files come from the official TMLR style repository, revision `7bf90efe3a0debbba703c05c43f3ff7e4d4a2992`:
https://github.com/JmlrOrg/tmlr-style-file

The repository Apache-2.0 license is preserved in `TMLR-STYLE-LICENSE.txt`. Neither review-mode template boilerplate nor preparation of a venue-specific PDF indicates an actual submission or acceptance.
